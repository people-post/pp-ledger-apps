# The Reserve app

The reserve holds the chain's supply. Its holders move tokens out of it: to the registrar, to
accounts, as agreed.

1. Under **New transfer**, the app shows the reserve's balance and how many of its holders must sign.
2. Type the account it goes to (its number: the registrar is 2), the amount, and the fee. The
   smallest fee the chain takes is filled in. Choose how many hours the signers have, at most a day
   unless the chain allows longer, and add notes for them if useful.
3. *Make the operation…* saves the file and opens it for review. Sign it yourself if you hold a
   reserve key.
4. Hand a copy to each other holder. Combine the signed copies they send back, then *Submit to the
   chain*.

Amounts are the chain's whole units, written with or without commas ("1,000,000").
